## Shape

lhp.c caps CSS numeric intake at `LHP_CSS_MAX_WHOLE` = 1e6 with the stated
intent (lhp.c:43-45) of keeping "the int32_t lws_fx_t whole part away from
overflow no matter how many digits the document offers". That holds for
*storage*. But the multiplier does its whole-part product in **int32
arithmetic** (libwebsockets.c:2562):

```c
w = a->whole * b->whole;          /* int32 x int32 -> int32 */
...
w += (int32_t)(_c1 / LWS_FX_FRACTION_MSD);
```

int32 overflows at products past 2^31 — operands of ~46342 each. With the
1e6 intake cap, two document-derived operands well inside the allowed range
(e.g. `width: 46342%` of a 46342px parent, or em chains
`font-size: 1000000` nested two levels) overflow the product: **signed
overflow UB** (immediate trap under the project's
`-fsanitize-undefined-trap-on-error` builds — the F-065/CID-913540 family)
and wrapped garbage magnitudes otherwise. The percent cascade
(`lws_css_compute_cascaded_length`, lhp.c:630-637) multiplies each level's
value by the accumulated length through exactly this path, up to its
20-level map; `lws_csp_px_base` percent×base is the same shape. The
`lws_fx_t` whole part itself also wraps at store time for products past
2^31 (int32 member).

Downstream is memory-safe (dlo box math bounds-checks against the surface,
the svg renderer saturates), so impact is the UB trap plus corrupted layout
— hostile-input-triggerable, violating the same hostile-safety discipline
the svg side was hardened for in F-064's fix.

## Attack path

Any lhp document consumer fed hostile CSS: a stylesheet (or inline style /
filter css) with legal-range but large values composed through percentage
or em chains reaches `lws_fx_mul` with two ≥ ~46k operands. One style rule
suffices (`width: 50000%` on an element inside a `width: 50000px`
ancestor).

## Recommendation

Stage the whole-part product in int64 (like the existing `_c1`/`_c2` frac
staging right below it) and saturate the result to ±INT32_MAX, mirroring
svg's `svg_qmul` discipline; or cap CSS intake at ~46341 so no product can
overflow (rendering-identical for real documents).
